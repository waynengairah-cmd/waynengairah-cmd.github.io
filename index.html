Tsavo Real Estate: Property Manager

The brief

Keep a remote, multi-site residential portfolio running from a central hub.

What I did

Ran rent and utility collections through the CRM, reconciled daily against accounts receivable, handled resident tickets by severity, and coordinated move-in and move-out readiness.

The result

Collections tracked to monthly targets with minimal arrears past deadlines, and exit registers feeding retention recommendations.

the backstory

Hi, I’m Wayne.

I’m a real estate professional in Nairobi. I studied Real Estate Management at Mount Kenya University (2020 to 2024, Second Class Upper, on a foundation scholarship), and my thesis on urban resilience through mixed-use developments was graded A.

Since then I’ve worked across property sales and research, pre-construction operations and compliance, and day-to-day residential management. I like the unglamorous parts: reconciliations, approvals, handover checklists, and the data that shows whether a building is working.
